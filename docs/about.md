# About the Systems Card Game

The Systems Card Game is a participatory method for making the structure and relationships within an operational system visible.

It can be used to explore systems such as livelihoods, communities, occupations, or other settings in which people, resources, environmental conditions, infrastructure, institutions, and decisions interact. Operational systems of non-human actors can be considered as well - one adaptation of the method is about frogs and their environments!


## Development of the method

The method began with Surprises and Dreams - an initial participatory workshop format for mapping livelihoods, exploring disruptions, and articulating desired futures. See Backstory for more details and acknowledgements. 

It has since been developed into a more general tool that can be adapted to different systems, questions, and workshop settings.


## What the tool supports

The card-based approach helps participants to:

- identify key elements of a system;
- discuss how those elements are connected;
- bring together different forms of knowledge and experience;
- examine how external pressures or unexpected events affect the system;
- explore possible responses and adaptations;
- articulate aspirations and desired futures, and what in the system needs to change, so that first steps towards those desired futures can be taken.

The physical arrangement of the cards creates a shared cognitive map that participants can discuss, revise, and use as the basis for further exercises.

Method supports open, constructive discussion, while making tensions between various aspirations visible. It is easy to learn and use, and it produces relevant material for research and decision-making.


## Backstory

The Systems Card Game started with Surprises and Dreams workshops, and reindeer husbandry card set. These were developed iteratively by a multidisciplinary research team throughout 2022. The deck evolved from handwritten cards made with a blank magician’s deck into a professionally printed card set available in several languages.
