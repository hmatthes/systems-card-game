# About the Systems Card Game

The Systems Card Game is a participatory method for making the structure and relationships within an operational system visible.

It can be used to explore systems such as livelihoods, communities, occupations, or other settings in which people, resources, environmental conditions, infrastructure, institutions, and decisions interact.

## Development of the method

The method began with *Playing with Dreams*, an initial participatory workshop format for mapping livelihoods, exploring disruptions, and articulating desired futures.

It has since been developed into a more general tool that can be adapted to different systems, questions, and workshop settings.

## What the tool supports

The card-based approach can help participants:

- identify important elements of a system;
- discuss how those elements are connected;
- bring together different forms of knowledge and experience;
- examine how external pressures or unexpected events affect the system;
- explore possible responses and adaptations;
- articulate aspirations and desired futures.

The physical arrangement of the cards creates a shared map that participants can discuss, revise, and use as the basis for further exercises.
