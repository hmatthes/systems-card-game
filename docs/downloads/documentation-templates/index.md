## 1. Choose what you are documenting

### Adaptations of the game

Use the **adaptation template** when you have created or modified a card set for a particular system, livelihood, place or topic.

The template covers:

- the system and purpose;
- intended users or participants;
- card categories and languages;
- changes made to the general tool;
- available materials;
- guidance for reuse;
- development, contacts, and references.


### Workshop or course report

Use the **workshop template** when you are documenting a particular workshop, course or other event in which a card set was used.

The template covers:

- the system and purpose;
- intended users or participants;
- card categories and languages;
- changes made to the general tool;
- available materials;
- guidance for reuse;
- development, contacts, and references.


## 2. Complete the template

You can use either format:

- Download and complete the Word template; or
- Open the online template, copy its contents into a document or Markdown file, and replace the instructions with your information.

You only need to complete one format.

### 3. Prepare the accompanying materials

Please include, where available:

- the completed documentation template;
- card sets or other printable materials intended for publication;
- facilitation guidelines, reports or teaching materials;
- one or more images for the example page;
- a caption, image description and photographer or image credit for each image;
- a preferred public contact name or link.

A public email address is optional. We will only display an email address when the contact person has explicitly agreed to this.

Please use clear filenames and tell us which description belongs to each file.

### Images, permissions and personal information

Only send material that may be published openly on this website and in its public GitHub repository.

Before submitting an image, please confirm that:

- you own the image or have permission from the photographer or rightsholder to publish it;
- recognizable people have agreed to its public online publication;
- any required parent or guardian permission has been obtained for recognizable children;
- you have provided the correct caption and credit.

Please do **not** upload consent forms, participant lists, private contact details or other confidential information. The submitting person or organisation should retain any consent records.

If permission to use a photograph is uncertain, you can instead provide an image of the cards, a system map or other workshop materials without recognizable people.

### 4. Send us your contribution

#### Submit through GitHub

[Submit an adaptation or workshop through GitHub](https://github.com/hmatthes/systems-card-game/issues/new?template=submit-example.yml){: .btn .btn-primary }

Attach the completed template and the files intended for publication. GitHub issues and their attachments are public, so do not include consent forms or confidential information.

#### Submit by email

If you do not use GitHub, send the completed template and accompanying materials to:

**[PROJECT EMAIL ADDRESS]**

For files that are too large for email, contact us first and we will agree on a suitable transfer method.

If you are already comfortable working with GitHub, you may also propose the completed pages through a pull request.

### What happens after submission?

The repository maintainers will:

1. check that the documentation and files are complete;
2. contact you about any missing information;
3. organise the material into the repository structure;
4. prepare the adaptation or workshop pages;
5. ask you to check the resulting pages where appropriate; and
6. publish the example on the website.

We may make small editorial changes for clarity and consistency, but we will not substantially change the meaning of your contribution without consulting you.
