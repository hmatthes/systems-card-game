# Reindeer husbandry operational environment

[Back to the adaptation overview]({{ site.baseurl }}/applications/reindeer-herding/)

## System or topic

This card set was developed to map and explore reindeer husbandry as a livelihood system in northern Fennoscandia and the Arctic.

## Purpose

The adaptation supports participants in:

- mapping the operational environment of reindeer husbandry;
- examining the effects of major drivers and disruptive developments;
- discussing possible responses and adaptations;
- identifying elements of desired futures for the livelihood;
- considering possible pathways towards those futures.

## Card categories

The cards describing the operational environment are divided into four categories:

1. **Pastures and land use**
2. **Reindeer herding and herders**
3. **Climate, the natural environment, and reindeer as animals**
4. **Economy, markets, society, and governance**

The set also includes joker cards representing major global drivers:

- accelerating climate change;
- climate-change mitigation and the green transition;
- a continuing war situation in Europe and its effects on costs;
- pandemics.

Smaller joker cards can be used to introduce more local or sudden developments.

## Languages

The cards are available in:

- Finnish;
- English;
- Swedish;
- Norwegian;
- Northern Sámi.

## How the general tool was adapted

The general card categories were specified to represent the operational environment of reindeer husbandry. The adaptation includes cards addressing the ecological, social, economic, political, and land-use dimensions of the livelihood.

Joker cards were added to support discussion of how large-scale drivers and sudden developments affect relationships within the mapped system. Participants can use these disruptions to explore consequences, responses, and adaptation options.

The workshop format developed around the card set also includes a “dreams” stage in which participants articulate elements of desired futures and discuss possible pathways towards them.
