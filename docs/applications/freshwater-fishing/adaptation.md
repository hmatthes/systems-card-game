# Commercial Fishing on Northern Lakes

[Back to the adaptation overview]({{ site.baseurl }}/applications/freshwater-fisheries/)

## System or topic
Commercial Fishing on Northern Lakes

## Purpose
Tool was made to better understand the operational environment of commercial fishers, operating on lakes in northern areas. Especially we were interested to discuss the changes in the operational environment and coping and adaptation of fishers and the livelihood. Tool has been developed keeping commercial lake fishing in northern Finland in mind, also in artificial lake settings, but it will work also with recreational fishing, in coastal/maritime environments, and with fisher communities elsewhere, with little adjustments. 

## Card categories
We have four categories: “Fish and the natural environment”, “Region, land use, actors; actions”, “Society, governance” and “Fishers and the livelihood”. We also have joker cards describing global developments (like climate change) and also more local surprises (like invasive species). Among the joker cards there are also concepts of “sustainability” and “adaptation”, to help the facilitation of workshop discussions.


## Languages
Finnish

## How the general tool was adapted 
Card categories and joker cards have been adapted to the livelihood in question. Among the joker cards there are also concepts of “sustainability” and “adaptation”, to help the facilitation of workshop discussions.

## Available materials
List of elements in the operational environment of nature tourism, as an excel sheet (56 elements and 9 jokers). Cards of these elements (77 cards, including some empty cards). When printing, and if using a pdf which has one card per sheet, it is best to print six (2x3, page orientation portrait) or nine pages per sheet (3x3, page orientation landscape).

## Development and contributors 
The deck of cards has been developed as part of the FROST -project (Arctic freshwater food systems: Influence of warming winters and increased snow cover; https://www.frost-lakes.com/), to be used in its workshops with lake fishers. The development has done in collaboration with researchers of the project ECOFI II (“Climate change impacts on Arctic ecosystems and associated climate feedbacks”). Involved people have been Minna Turunen, Sirpa Rasmus, Hannah Strauss-Mazzullo and Helena Syrjänen (University of Lapland) and Sanna-Mari Suopajärvi (Regional Council of Lapland, MountResilience -project). Asa-Marie Kultima (University of Lapland) produced the physical version of the card deck.
