# Workshop with Danish Secondary School students at DMI, Copenhagen, Denmark

[Back to the workshop overview]({{ site.baseurl }}/applications/being-a-frog/workshops/Danish-secondary-school/)

## Adapted card set used

The workshop used the **Being a Frog** card set. Its system cards represent Livelihoods and Community, Climate and Environment, Infrastructure as well as Society, Economy and Governance.

[Read the card-set documentation]({{ site.baseurl }}/applications/being-a-frog/adaptation/)

## Date and location
Danish Meteorological Institute, Copenhage, Denmark in January 2026

## Organisers and facilitators 
This card game was developed at the Danish Meteorological Institute by Anette Ester Dissing, Ruth Mottram and Kristiina Verro.

## Participants 
Secondary school students, grades 7-9.

## Purpose
The workshop aimed to make the kids reflect on system interconnections (environmental and climate related) especially their everyday life and surroundings at their local community. Purpose was to make kids aware of their own effect and response on their surroundings. To make them map connections and find interconnections they were not aware of. Furthermore to let then exploring how these mapped systems respond to climate-driven changes.

## Outcomes and observations
Main insights: 
Kids reflected on the interconnections and links between somehow wery diffent elements in a system.
They learned what is a system? And what are disruptions. How can they as individuals and as a community affect their environment and surroundings. 

follow-up decisions or actions:
At this level there need to be much time addressed to setting the frame, eg describing and explaining what is a system. Defining elements the participants have not heard of yet.
