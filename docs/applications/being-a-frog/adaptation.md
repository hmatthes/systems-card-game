# Operational environment for being a frog

[Back to the adaptation overview]({{ site.baseurl }}/applications/being-a-frog/)

## System or topic

This adaptation is a mapping tool for living conditions of being a frog. 

## Purpose

This adaptation was made to make the kids reflect on system interconnections (environmental and climate related) especially their everyday life and surroundings at their local community. 

## Card categories 

We used four different categories for cards: **Livelihoods, community**, **Climate, environment**, **Infrastructure** and **Society, economy, governance**.

## Languages 

This card set is available in Danish.

## Guidance for reuse 

The adaptation is designed for facilitated discussions in groups of approximately five to eight people.
