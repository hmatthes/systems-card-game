# Greenland/Ilulissat Livelihoods

This adaptation of the Systems Card Game was developed to explore the community of livelihoods in Ilulissat, Greenland, and its connections with infrastructure, society and the environment.

Developed for Avannaata Kommunia, the card set helps community members and polar researchers create a shared system map, examine how climate-driven changes might affect it, and discuss possible pathways towards a climate-resilient future.

## About this adaptation

The cards represent local livelihoods and community life, climate and environmental conditions, infrastructure, society, economy and governance.

A separate category of disruption cards introduces developments such as extreme weather, permafrost thaw, thinner sea ice, changing seasons, geopolitical tensions and pandemics.

The adaptation is available in English and Kalaallisut.

## How it is used

The workshop process consists of three connected stages:

1. Participants create a shared map of the local livelihood environment.
2. The group explores the effects of a climate-related surprise or disruption and considers possible responses.
3. Participants identify personal and collective dreams and discuss desirable futures for the community.

The process combines locally grounded knowledge and experience with information from climate scientists.
