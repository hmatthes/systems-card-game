# Workshop at the PolarRES ECR Bootcamp, Søminestationen, Holbæk, Denmark

[Back to the workshop overview]({{ site.baseurl }}/applications/being-a-scientist/workshops/PolarRES-bootcamp-2023/)

## Adapted card set used* 

The workshop used the **Operational environment for being a scientist** card set. Its system cards represent Conditions for participation in science, Rules and recognition, Scientific work practice, and Competition and Access.

[Read the card-set documentation]({{ site.baseurl }}/applications/being-a-scientist/adaptation/)

## Date and location* 
Søminestationen, Holbæk, Denmark in July 2023

## Organisers* and facilitators 
The bootcamp was organized by the PolarRES project for its early career researchers. The workshop was organized as a side event of the bootcamp by Heidrun Matthes. There was one facilitator.

## Participants* 
Around 15 early career researchers participated in the workshop, all of them either PhD students or PostDocs. The workshop was organized as a side event of the bootcamp.

## Purpose* 
The workshop was organized as a participatory pilot exploring how the systems card game can be adapted to livelihoods that are not environment dependend. The main goal was to find out which components the operational system of being a scientist could have. It was also aiming at demosntrating the use of the tool to natural scientists. The idea was to spark a discussion on career path choices and what is needed to be successful as a scientist.

## Workshop format and duration
Participants were introduced to the general idea of system mapping, and the potential categories that could be applied to the operational system of being a scientist. Then a round the table exersice asked participants for system components, and which category they might belong to. After the system components were established, participants were asked to point out the componenet they saw as most critical for being successful as a scientist.

## Outcomes and observations
Participants at different career levels had different ideas about what the critical components of the system for being successful as a scientist would be. Later career participants (PostDocs in more senior positions) were more likely to choose professional networks as the most critical component, while PhD students and early career PostDocs (PostDocs on their first position) tended to see time management/work-life balance as the most critical component.
