# How to use the Systems Card Game
# Advertisement on the game:

Join us for a climate-resilient, future-focused workshop to explore how Ilulissat livelihoods can adapt and thrive over the long term.
Using the group card game Playing With Dreams, we will co-create climate-aware stories about Ilulissat’s future to help communities and participants cope with possible changes and prepare for future climate scenarios.

In this workshop, we will explore:
- The local ways of living
- How past, present, and future changes are connected
- How to respond and adapt to unexpected climate events
- What kind of future communities want to create together

The workshop is facilitated by climate scientists from the Danish Meteorological Institute, but you and your community are at the heart of the conversation — your voices and experiences guide the game. Let's imagine and build a climate-smart future together!


# Quick explainer:

Welcome to the climate resilient future-focused workshop Playing With Dreams, where we will explore how life in Ilulissat can adapt and thrive in the future. It is played in a  group of 5–8 people, and guided by a facilitator. 

### Mapping: 
Using the game deck, participants will take turns selecting 2–4 keyword cards each and work together to create a “map” of the community’s livelihood environment. They will use markers to draw connections and show relationships between the elements.
What to think about: Which keywords matter most to me? Which cards should be placed next to each other? Which cards are central in the map? Which cards are linked?
Not sure what a card means? Just ask — your facilitator and group are here for you! 
	Can’t find a card that fits? Write your own keyword!
### Surprises and Preparedness: 
Your group livelihood map is ready! Now each group will create a response to a “surprise” climate card.
What to think about: What does this climate event mean? How do the changes in climate or unexpected events affect the map? How can your group community cope, adapt, and prepare?
            Not sure what this climate event means? Ask the scientist!

### Dreams and Futures: 
In this portion of the workshop, each participant reflects on their personal dreams related to their livelihood. 
Dreams and aspirations weave a vision of the future we want. Meaningful change happens when there is a vision to strive toward; articulating these dreams can be both empowering for the individual and inspiring for a community.

**Communities make the best progress when everyone’s input is valued and heard. Each of us brings different strengths and perspectives, and by combining them, we can come up with better ideas and solutions together. Let’s stay open, respectful, constructive, and supportive..**

# Message for the facilitators:
- One general facilitator should be in charge of opening and closing the event, keeping time, and guiding the agenda. 
- For every table, there should be one facilitator in charge of guiding the conversations and shepherding their group through the exercises. Make sure everyone in the group is heard and included in the conversation. If the conversation becomes too heated or stuck, try to bring attention to another connection on the map.

**Please start with a general message of respect:**
Let’s keep our discussion respectful and collaborative — communities make the best progress when everyone’s input is valued and heard. Each of us brings different strengths and perspectives, and by combining them, we can come up with better ideas and solutions together. Let’s stay open, constructive, and supportive..
