In the workshop students themselves selected the set of key elements, and the cards were prepared after that (selected elements were written on tape, and sticked to cards). Of the listed 120 elements, 20-30 were selected for the actual workshop. From those cards, students built the operational environment of nature tourism as one group (session I), and discussed the impacts of climate change (session II).

The workshop used the **Operational environment of nature tourism** card set. Its system cards represent “Business”, “Environment and climate”, “Economy, markets, society, governance”, “Land use”, and “Tourists”.
